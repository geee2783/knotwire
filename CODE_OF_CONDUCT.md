# Code of Conduct

Knotwire follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

- Be welcoming and inclusive. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.
- Critique ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
- Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban contributors who
  violate these principles.

Report violations privately via security@knotwire.dev (placeholder until GA).
